NOX

Customer enquiries recorded from a web form and classified with AI. A visitor contacts us through the form on the landing page; n8n receives the request and an AI classifies it (category, priority, sentiment); FastAPI encrypts it with AES-256-GCM before storing it in PostgreSQL, and the team handles it in a Vue 3 dashboard.
